PhD position

Computational chemistry and materials science

I am looking for a PhD student to join my group in the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge, with an expected start date of October 2027.

The position is funded by my ERC Starting Grant QUANTEX, “Quantum Theory of the Periodic Tables Under Extreme Conditions”. The project will investigate how extreme pressure changes electronic structure, chemical bonding, and chemical periodicity, combining first-principles calculations with crystal structure prediction, machine-learning methods, and chemical bonding analysis.

I welcome applications from candidates interested in theoretical chemistry, condensed matter physics, and computational materials science. A strong interest in theoretical and solid-state chemistry is essential.
